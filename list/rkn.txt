# Probe panel for the strategy checker: every candidate strategy is scored by
# how many of these domains open through it. Panel size multiplies straight
# into the cost of a check, so a slow domain costs as much as a fast one and
# contributes exactly one bit either way.

# These are the 100 fastest of the 107 measured on 2026-08-04 over WARP
# (wireproxy-awg, WARPv2_53), three trials each, median time from opening the
# socket to complete HTTP response headers. Measuring over WARP rather than
# direct is deliberate: direct timings are shaped by whatever the ISP is doing
# to each host, which is the variable under test.

# would score every strategy as a failure), and greenpeace.org,
